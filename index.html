Origins & Tradition

How Corqua Was Born

01

The Finland Trip

We travelled to Finland from school for a pitch competition. There we noticed everyday hydration problems up close, in both plastic and steel bottles.

02

Two Countries, One Problem

In cold Finland, plastic bottle users complained they couldn't hold hot water or keep temperature steady. Back in India, people said their water got too hot, and steel bottle users disliked the weight and the space they take up.

03

The Win

We built Corqua (C-O-R-Q-U-A) to solve this, pitched it, and won the competition, receiving ₹1.2 lakh in prize money to start the company.

04

Today

Corqua is in active prototype testing in India, preparing its first batch for pioneers committed to natural, plastic-free living.

Material Science

Crafted Entirely by Nature

01

Zero Microplastics

Traditional steel and plastic bottles leach microplastics into water. Corqua uses 100% organic cellular cork and natural beeswax barrier.

02

Natural Insulation

Cork’s natural cellular structure contains over 40 million microscopic air cells per cubic cm, creating thermal stability naturally.

03

Self-Healing Barrier

Organic beeswax possesses natural antimicrobial properties that prevent bacterial growth while ensuring a perfectly smooth hydrophobic seal.
